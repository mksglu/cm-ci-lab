1. **Swapped values across sibling models.** `deepseek-v4-flash` goes 943,717 → 131,072. `deepseek-v4-flash-0731` goes 131,072 → 943,718. `deepseek-v4.1-flash` goes 943,718 → 131,072. `~z-ai/glm-latest` goes 943,718 → 131,072. 2. **Derived values that look like a ratio.** Numbers like 943,717/943,718 (about 90% of 1,048,576), 117,964 (90% of 131,072), 235,929 (90% of 262,144) and 945,000 are most likely a fallback computed as 90% of context, not real limits. Several of the changes replace these with plausible real caps (16,384 for Gemma, 131,072 for DeepSeek). Those are improvements. But `deepseek-v4-flash-0731` (943,718) and `xiaomi/mimo-v2.5-pro` (235,929) still carry the derived values, so they are probably still wrong.
3. **Possible regression.** `qwen/qwen3.5-27b` rises from 65,536 to 81,920. This is plausible, but unverified.
4. **Minor, from the diff alone.** `z-ai/glm-5.1` falls from 131,072 to 128,000, which looks like a rounding of the real limit.

Wrong `max_output_tokens` values matter. Anything that uses profiles to default or validate output length could set a limit the provider rejects, or cap output too low. The 0731 DeepSeek entry is the most likely to cause a provider-side error.

### New model entries
- `openai/gpt-6.1-terra` and `-pro` are identical apart from the name. That's plausible, but worth confirming against the source.

### Suggestions

- Have the bot's PR summary flag large swings, such as more than 5x, for human review.
